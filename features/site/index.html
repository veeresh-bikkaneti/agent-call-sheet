              How to play

              Mouse and keyboard

              • Left drag sculpts, carves or paints with the current tool. Click to place.
              • Right drag turns the camera. Middle drag or Shift + left drag pans.
              • Wheel zooms. W A S D or the arrows pan. Q and E turn.
              • 1 to 7 pick a tool. B opens the build tray. R rotates. Ctrl + D duplicates. Delete removes.
              • Ctrl + Z undoes. Ctrl + Y or Ctrl + Shift + Z redoes. G toggles grid snap. [ and ] size the brush.
              • P opens photo mode. Esc pauses.

              Controller

              • Left stick moves the cursor and the camera. Right stick turns and tilts.
              • A applies or places. B goes back. X rotates. Y removes.
              • LB and RB change the tool. LT and RT zoom.
              • D-pad left and right picks a build item. Up and down change the brush.
              • View undoes. Menu pauses. Pressing the right stick opens photo mode.

              Touch (landscape)

              • One finger uses the current tool.
              • Two fingers drag to pan, pinch to zoom and twist to turn.

              The oasis

              Water runs from the spring and from wells along the canals you carve, and it cannot climb more than a little. Plantings near flowing water thrive. Every goal you finish opens a new district. There is no way to lose.

              About

              Saffron Spring is the landing page of The Agent Call Sheet, an AI-agent skill and persona library. The game, its art, its sound and its music are original and made procedurally in the browser with three.js and the Web Audio API.

              Try the kit

              git clone https://github.com/veeresh-bikkaneti/agent-call-sheet.git
cd agent-call-sheet
python3 features/paperclip-bridge/bootstrap.py --list

              The last command previews what the bootstrap would copy and writes nothing. The beginner's guide walks through the rest.

              three.js is MIT licensed and served from this site. Nothing is loaded from other sites, and nothing you do is sent anywhere. No generative AI images or models were used: every shape, colour and sound is made by code.
