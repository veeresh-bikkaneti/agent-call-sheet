Walkthrough

Walkthrough video

A 24-second clip, with sound. Before you press play, one honest note about what it shows.

This clip is not a demo of the kit. It is a recording of Saffron Spring, an earlier desert-settlement game prototype that used to be this site's front page. It is a tour of that game's oasis village in "golden hour" light. It does not show installing the kit, a crew run or the live board. We kept it because it is the only walkthrough video this site has, and a replacement that shows the real kit has not been recorded yet.

Download the video (MP4, about 4 MB)

What is in the video

The poster frame shows a small desert settlement from above, with flat-roofed houses, tall wind towers, palm trees, a market awning, and a small blue pool, with a caption reading "Golden hour". The clip is 24 seconds long and has an audio track. That is all we can say from the files in the repo; we have not written a scene-by-scene description beyond that.

Captions and transcript: a known gap

There are no captions and no transcript for this video. That is a known accessibility gap. The clip has no spoken dialogue we have documented, and we have not invented a transcript. Everything this site teaches is written out on its pages, so the video is not needed for any step. If you rely on captions, please skip it; you lose nothing. A replacement video, with captions, would be the proper fix.
