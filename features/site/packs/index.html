Included versus link-only

Every entry is either included (its files were copied into this repo) or link-only (nothing copied; the manifest points you to the author's home repo, and you fetch it there under its own license). A few entries are practices: ways of working with no file to copy or link.

Distribution was decided on license evidence, entry by entry. An entry was only included where one of these held: a permissive license file in the folder (MIT or Apache-2.0); MIT stated in the kit catalog; MIT declared in the skill's own frontmatter; a permissive license at the upstream repo root, verified at a pinned commit; or the material is our own. Everything doubtful went link-only. Link-only is not a lesser status; it is the honest one where copying was not evidenced as permitted.

Licensing and attribution

Our own original material (the starter prompt, the house rules, the Crew Orchestrator skill, agents-orchestrator and production-path-verification) is under the Apache License 2.0. The NOTICE file in the repo says exactly what that covers.

The root license does not cover third-party files. Most of this library is other people's work. It keeps its own license and its credit, recorded per entry in the manifest (author, source, license, and the evidence we relied on). Nothing in the repo relicenses anyone else's work.

When in doubt about an entry, read its row in the manifest before using it. And note that a license is not a safety review: some skills contain scripts, so read before you install.
