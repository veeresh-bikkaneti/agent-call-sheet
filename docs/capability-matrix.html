How to read this page

Every cell carries one of three labels. There are no bare checkmarks or crosses on this page.

Verified — the statement in the cell was run, and the cell says what was run, on which account or plan (or that there was none), and on what date. A Verified cell can also verify an absence: when a run proved a capability is not there, the cell says so in words.

Attributed — the claim comes from the vendor's own documentation, not from a run of ours. The cell says “vendor docs”, gives the date those docs were read, and says “not run-tested”.

Unverified — we have no evidence in our source reports that is strong enough to support a claim. The word stands alone in the cell, on purpose: it is the honest cell, not a missing one.

Of the 55 cells below: 21 Verified, 3 Attributed, 31 Unverified.
