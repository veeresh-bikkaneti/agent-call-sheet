Watch the events in 3D

We walk through one crew run, step by step, in a small 3D scene.

A visual demo — a cartoon of how a crew run flows. It is not the tool itself, and it proves nothing by itself; the real record of a run is its session record.

Motion is reduced on your device, so we do not play or tween (move smoothly) anything here. The buttons step through the events, and each step jumps straight to that event’s end pose. The captions carry the meaning either way.

Ready. Press Play, or pick an event below. We start with the crew scattered, and we end with a report to you, the human.

The events, in words

The 3D scene could not start on this device, so here are the events as text. The meaning is the same:

  1. Standup — the crew gathers and states the target.
  2. Casting — work is handed out on task cards.
  3. Build — the builder builds.
  4. Test — the work is tested.
  5. The gate, part 1 — the Reality Checker starts at “needs work” — a route that isn’t proven bounces off.
  6. The gate, part 2 — proven work passes, marked READY.
  7. Report — the crew reports the exact final state; anything irreversible waits for the human’s word.

How to read the scene: the six labelled figures are the crew seats. The Reality Checker stands at the gate (an arch) because that seat checks the work before it can pass. The marker at the front is you, the human — the person who decides anything that cannot be undone.
