Picture 1

The parts, and how they connect

  1. A human brings a job.
  2. The human enters through the front door. The front door is the way in.
  3. Inside the front door, the starter prompt sets the house rules. The house rules are the rules we work by.
  4. Also inside the front door, the Crew Orchestrator is the coordinator. The coordinator runs the job.
  5. The coordinator casts seats from the library. To cast a seat means to pick the right persona or skill for one part of the job.
  6. The library holds 6 packs: Crew Personas (41), Rigor (17), Memory & Comms (3), Design / Video / 3D (45), SEO (33), and Legal (2).
  7. The work happens on task cards. Each card holds one piece of work.
  8. Underneath everything, the Manifest spans the library. It is the record of who made each entry, its source, and its license evidence. It also records whether we included the files or linked only.

Picture 2

How one job flows

We run every job in the same order. These are the Crew Orchestrator phases, in order. We do not skip a phase.

  1. Confirm the target. We agree what the job is, and what done looks like.
  2. Plan. We write the steps, and we name who does each step.
  3. Build. We do the work, on task cards.
  4. Test. We check the work against the target.
  5. Reality Checker gate. This is a gate. A gate is a check that must pass before work moves on. The Reality Checker is a separate checker. The coordinator never certifies its own work. The checker starts at NEEDS WORK. At the diamond, there are two ways out: not proven loops back to Build, with the failure attached, so we fix the right thing; proven goes on.
  6. Report. We report what we did, what we checked, and what we did not check.
  7. The human decides anything irreversible. Irreversible means it cannot be undone with ease. We do not do those steps without the human's explicit word.

We repeat the loop as needed. Work only moves on from the gate when it is proven.
