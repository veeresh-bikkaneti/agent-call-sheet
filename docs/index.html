What this kit is

We keep a library. In it, we collect two kinds of helpers for AI work.

A persona is a named role. It has one point of view and one job to do. Think of it as a seat at a table, with a clear reason to be there.

A skill is a set of instructions we can reuse. It tells an AI helper how to do one kind of work, step by step.

We also built a front door. That is the way in. It is how a person starts, and how we ask for the right helpers for a job. It includes the starter prompt and the Crew Orchestrator, and we explain both on the next page.

So, in plain words: this kit is a library of AI-agent skills and personas, plus a front door to use them. We keep a record for every entry in the library. That record shows who made it, where it came from, and why we hold it the way we do.

Plain definitions

Words we use

We use a few short words a lot. Here is what each one means, in plain words.

Orchestrator
The coordinator. It takes a job in at the front door, casts the seats, and moves the work through each phase.
Task card
One piece of work, written on one card. The work happens on task cards, so we can see what is done and what is left.
Standup
A short check-in. We say what is done, what is next, and what is blocked, in a few lines.
Gate
A check that must pass before work moves on. If it does not pass, the work goes back.
Reality Checker
A separate checker who trusts only re-measured evidence. The checker starts at NEEDS WORK, and only evidence moves the verdict.
Manifest
Our record of the library. For each entry, it holds who made it, its source, and its license evidence, plus the choice to include files or link only.
Council
A review panel. Each seat attacks a change from one angle, so we miss less.
