      Field notes

      What this console is, what it deliberately does not do, and what was verified for you. Kept honest on purpose.

      What it does

      • Reads the gate verdict, eval results and manifest, and re-probes each finding against the working tree.
      • Renders a procedural container terminal — boxes, gantry crane, wet yard, drizzle — from Three.js primitives. No model files.
      • Keeps your triage state, loadout, sound and mode in this browser only.

      What it does not do

      • It runs nothing. No Python, no shell, no server, no network call except the pinned Three.js modules you opt into.
      • It ships no recorded audio and no GLTF models. Interface sounds are synthesised in the browser and are off by default.
      • Nothing here is live. The data was baked from — when the page was built.
      • Triaging a finding is a reading gesture, not a fix. The status comes from the probe.
